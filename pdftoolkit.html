Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Welcome to PDF Toolkit. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Key Point: PDF Toolkit is designed with privacy in mind. All PDF processing happens locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal files or documents to any external servers.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Files and Documents

PDF Toolkit processes PDF files and images that you select on your device. These files are:

  • Processed locally on your device only
  • Never uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers
  • Stored in your device's local storage in the Downloads/PDFToolkit folder
  • Under your complete control - you can delete them anytime

1.2 Device Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Storage Access: To read input files and save processed PDFs
  • Internet: For displaying ads (free version) and processing in-app purchases

We do not access any other data on your device.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

To improve app stability and understand how features are used, we collect limited diagnostic data through Google Firebase:

  • Crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics): stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and a randomly-generated installation ID. Used only to diagnose and fix crashes.
  • Anonymous usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics): session counts, which tools are opened, and basic device information. Used only to understand which features are useful. Not linked to your identity.

This data never includes the contents of your PDFs or any personally identifying information. You can disable analytics in your Google account settings.

2. Third-Party Services

2.1 Facebook Audience Network (Ads)

The free version of PDF Toolkit displays ads through Facebook Audience Network. Facebook may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data

Facebook's data collection is subject to their own privacy policy. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings. Pro users do not see any ads.

Facebook Audience Network Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy

2.2 Google Play Billing

For in-app purchases and subscriptions, we use Google Play's billing system. Google handles all payment processing and may collect:

  • Transaction information
  • Purchase history
  • Google account information

We do not store or have access to your payment information. All transactions are processed securely by Google Play.

Google Play Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.3 Firebase (Google)

We use Google Firebase for crash reporting (Crashlytics) and anonymous analytics. Firebase may collect:

  • Crash stack traces and device state at time of crash
  • App version, OS version, device model
  • A randomly-generated installation ID
  • Anonymous session and screen-view events

No PDF contents are ever sent to Firebase. Crash and analytics data is encrypted in transit.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2.4 Google ML Kit (OCR)

The OCR Scan feature uses Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The text recognition model runs entirely on your device — your images and PDFs are never uploaded for OCR processing. The ML Kit library may report anonymous usage telemetry (e.g. model version, success / failure) to Google.

ML Kit Terms & Privacy: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

3. How We Use Your Information

The limited data we do collect is used as follows:

  • File Processing: Your files are processed locally to perform PDF operations (view, merge, split, compress, rotate, watermark, protect, sign, OCR, and convert)
  • Storage: Processed files are saved to your device's local app folder, accessible via Recent Files inside the app
  • Crash Reports: Diagnostic data is used only to identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data helps us decide which features to improve next
  • Purchases: Google Play tracks your subscription status to unlock Pro features

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your information. Your PDF and image files never leave your device. The only data shared with third parties is:

  • To Facebook (Audience Network): Anonymous ad-related data such as advertising ID and ad interactions (free users only)
  • To Google (Firebase Crashlytics): Crash reports and basic device info to help us fix bugs
  • To Google (Firebase Analytics): Anonymous usage events (e.g. which tools are opened)
  • To Google (ML Kit): Anonymous telemetry about the on-device OCR model only — never any image or extracted text
  • To Google (Play Billing): Purchase verification data when you buy Pro

Each of these is governed by their respective privacy policies linked above.

5. Data Security

Your data security is ensured by:

  • Local Processing: All operations happen on your device
  • No Cloud Storage: We don't store files on external servers
  • Android Security: Files are protected by your device's security features
  • No Accounts: No login required, no user accounts, no passwords to secure

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 File Control

  • Delete Files: Remove any processed PDFs from Downloads/PDFToolkit folder anytime
  • Uninstall App: Removes all app data (but not your saved PDFs)

6.2 Advertising Choices

  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Upgrade to Pro: Remove all ads permanently

6.3 Subscription Management

  • Cancel Anytime: Play Store → Subscriptions → PDF Toolkit Pro
  • Restore Purchases: Use "Restore Purchases" button in app settings

7. Children's Privacy

PDF Toolkit does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is rated 18+ due to advertising content. We recommend parental supervision for younger users.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. International Users

PDF Toolkit is available globally. All PDF and image processing happens locally on your device, so your files are never transferred internationally. Limited diagnostic data (crash reports, anonymous analytics) is transmitted to Google Firebase servers, and ad-related data is transmitted to Facebook servers. These transfers are governed by Google's and Facebook's respective privacy policies and are encrypted in transit.

10. Legal Compliance

PDF Toolkit complies with:

  • GDPR (European Union)
  • CCPA (California)
  • Other Privacy Laws: Applicable data protection regulations worldwide

We collect only the limited diagnostic data disclosed above (crash reports and anonymous analytics). We do not collect any personally identifying information, and your PDF files always stay on your device. We maintain transparency and user control as a matter of policy.
