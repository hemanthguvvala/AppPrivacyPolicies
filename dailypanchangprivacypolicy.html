Daily Panchang

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 May 2026

Daily Panchang (the “app”) is a Hindu calendar and panchang utility published by Hemanth Guvvala (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. The short version: we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and does not connect to any server.

1. Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no remote crash reporting, and no telemetry. All panchang calculations (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, choghadiya, rahu kaal, sunrise, sunset, eclipses, sankrantis) are performed entirely on your device using astronomical formulas built into the app. No data leaves your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it:

  • Your selected city preset (e.g. Hyderabad, Mumbai)
  • Your selected language and theme
  • Your sunrise notification preference
  • Your selected vrat reminders (Ekadashi, Pradosh, Sankashti, Purnima / Amavasya)
  • Your selected Ekadashi tradition (Smarta or Vaishnava)
  • Your selected lunar month system (Amanta or Purnimanta)
  • Your birth nakshatra, if you set one to personalize Tarabala and Chandrabala
  • A small in-memory cache of recently computed panchang values
  • A local crash log file (used only for your own troubleshooting; never transmitted anywhere)

This data is wiped when you uninstall the app or clear app data from Android Settings.

3. Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — only used if you opt in to the daily sunrise notification.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — reschedules your sunrise notification after a device reboot, so the daily reminder keeps working without you reopening the app.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — optional. Only requested if you tap “Use my location” during onboarding. We read your device’s last-known coarse location once, match it to the nearest city in the app’s built-in city list, and discard the coordinates. The location is never stored or transmitted. You can deny this permission and search for your city manually instead.

The app does not request internet, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, calendar, or precise-location access. The app makes no network connections of any kind during normal use.

4. Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no other third-party SDKs. Earlier development builds used Google AdMob; this has been removed from all released versions of the app.

5. Festival and panchang data

Festival dates, eclipse dates, and panchang labels shipped with the app are bundled as static data files at install time. No festival or calendar data is fetched from any server while the app is in use.

6. Opening this Privacy Policy

When you tap the “Privacy policy” row in the app’s Settings, the app opens this URL in your default web browser. That request is handled by your browser, not by the app, and is subject to your browser’s and network provider’s privacy practices.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing for us to delete if a child has used it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
