Daily Panchang

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Daily Panchang (the “app”) is a Hindu calendar and panchang utility published by Hemanth Guvvala (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. The short version: we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data ourselves.

1. Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no telemetry. All panchang calculations (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, choghadiya, rahu kaal, sunrise, sunset) are performed entirely on your device using astronomical formulas built into the app.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it:

  • Your selected city preset (e.g. Hyderabad, Mumbai)
  • Your selected language and theme
  • Your sunrise notification preference
  • A small in-memory cache of recently computed panchang values

This data is wiped when you uninstall the app or clear app data from Android Settings.

3. Permissions

  • INTERNET — required by Google Mobile Ads to load advertisements (see §4).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — only used if you opt in to the daily sunrise notification.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — reschedules your sunrise notification after a device reboot.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — used by Google Mobile Ads as described below.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, or camera access.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob is a Google product and operates under Google’s privacy policy. AdMob may collect or use:

  • Your Android Advertising ID (resettable from Android Settings → Privacy → Ads)
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP address
  • Device and ad-interaction information

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We never see it. To learn more or to opt out of personalized ads, see Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from ads
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID

5. Children’s privacy

The app is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. AdMob ads served in the app may be subject to family-policy restrictions per Google’s configuration.

6. Festival and panchang data

Festival dates and panchang labels shipped with the app are bundled as static data files at install time. No festival or calendar data is fetched from any server while the app is in use.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
