Gully Cricket Scorer — Privacy Policy

The simplest way to score amateur cricket. Your match data never leaves your phone.

Effective date: 27 August 2026 · App: Gully Cricket Scorer (com.hemanthguvvala.gullycricketscorer) · Developer: Hemanth Guvvala

Short version. Your cricket data never leaves your phone. Team names, players, scores and match history are stored only on your device, are not uploaded anywhere, and there is no account to create. The app does show ads, and Google's ad system collects some device information in order to serve them — that is the only data collection here, it is described in full in section 5, and it is entirely separate from your match data.

1. What information we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. There is no account, no login, and no server of ours. We never ask for your email, phone number or name, and we operate no database that holds anything about you.

Our advertising provider does collect data. The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, and to do that Google receives information from your device including your advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ads. We do not receive this information; Google does. Section 5 covers it in detail and links to Google's own policy.

The match data you enter inside the app — team names, player names, scores, ball-by-ball events, and match history — is saved only on your device's internal storage in private app files. It is not synced, uploaded, or backed up to any service we operate.

2. Permissions we request

The app requests two permissions, both solely for advertising:

  • INTERNET — so the ad system can fetch ads. The scoring, history and statistics features make no network requests whatsoever; your match data is never uploaded, not even to us.
  • AD_ID — access to the device advertising ID, which Android requires apps to declare when they show ads. You can reset or delete this ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

We request no location, camera, contacts, microphone or storage permissions.

When you tap “Share to WhatsApp” or any other share action, the app hands a generated scorecard image to Android's standard share sheet. Where that image goes next is governed by the app you choose (WhatsApp, email, etc.) and its own privacy policy — not ours.

3. How we use information

We perform no profiling or tracking of our own, and we never read your match data. Advertising targeting, where it happens, is carried out by Google using the data described in section 1 — and in regions where consent is required, only after you have agreed to it (see section 6).

4. How we share information

We never share, sell or rent your match data — we could not, as it is only on your phone. The only third party receiving any data is Google, as the advertising provider described in section 5, and that exchange happens directly between your device and Google.

5. Third-party services and SDKs

Google AdMob (advertising). The app displays banner ads on the Matches, Stats and More screens, and one full-screen ad after a completed match. Google may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interactions, and may use this to personalise the ads you see. This is governed by how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy. There is no analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK or social login SDK in the app.

Google User Messaging Platform (consent). Where the law requires it — the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — you are shown a consent form before any personalised ad is served, and your choice is stored on your device. You can change it at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app.

To opt out of personalised ads anywhere in the world, use Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

The app includes one Google library: the Google Play In-App Review API. After you finish a match, the app may ask the Google Play Store app on your device to display its own rating card. We do not receive the rating, we are not told whether the card was shown, and none of your match data is sent or attached. Any review you choose to leave is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

The Cricket gear screen, and the gear cards shown on the home screen, the over break, the scorecard and under More, contain Amazon Associates affiliate links. Tapping one opens Amazon in your browser or the Amazon app with a search term and our associate tag. We may earn a commission on a resulting purchase, at no extra cost to you. No information about your matches, players or scores is included in that link. Once you leave, Amazon’s own privacy policy governs what they collect.

If a future release of the app ever integrates such a service, this privacy policy will be updated before that release, and the updated effective date will be displayed at the top of this page.

6. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The app is not directed at children, and its advertising is not configured for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising described above, please contact us using the details in section 10 and we will help you raise it with Google.

7. Data security

Match data is stored in your device's private app sandbox, accessible only to the Gully Cricket Scorer app and protected by your device's standard operating-system security model. Because nothing is transmitted off the device, there is no network-side data to secure.

If you back up your device (Google Drive auto-backup, manual file backup, etc.), match data may be included in your backup — those backups are governed by the backup provider's terms, not ours.

8. Your choices and rights

You always have full control over your match data:

  • Open Settings → Clear all data inside the app to permanently delete every match, every player record, and every saved setting on the device.
  • Uninstall the app from Android Settings to remove all of its data (active match, archived matches, settings) immediately and irrevocably.
  • Use Settings → Export backup to save a copy of your data as a JSON file you control. You can delete that file at any time.

Because we do not hold any of your data on our side, there is no “delete my account” or “export my data” request for us to process — everything is in your hands directly.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new effective date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes (for example, the introduction of any third-party data collection) will be highlighted at the top of the policy and announced in the app's release notes on the Google Play Store.

10. Contact

If you have a question about this privacy policy, the app's data practices, or anything else, please write to hemanth.guvvala@ibsplc.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.
